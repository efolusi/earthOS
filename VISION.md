# Vision: EarthOS

**A real-time interactive 3D digital twin of Earth, in your browser, open to everyone.**

## The problem

Planetary data (weather, satellites, flights, ships, seismic activity) exists in silos behind specialist tools and paid APIs. Seeing Earth as one living system should not require a GIS degree or an enterprise license.

## Our answer

EarthOS renders the planet as a live, explorable 3D globe in the browser: no install, no account, no paywall. Open-source (MIT), published on npm, deployable by anyone.

## Why open-source

This is a product where openness makes it stronger. A digital twin of Earth benefits from contributors adding data layers, from transparency in how data is rendered, and from distribution that only free software gets. Efolusi supports open-source where openness compounds value; EarthOS is our flagship example.

## The gap we own

Existing globe products are closed-source, and enterprise digital-twin platforms are sold B2B. Our bet: no giant will ship a free, open, hackable, real-time Earth in the browser, because there is no enterprise contract in it. That is exactly the gap a studio like us can own.

## Where this fits in Efolusi

## What success looks like

EarthOS becomes the default open canvas for putting planetary-scale data on a globe: used by hobbyists, educators, and developers who fork it into things we never imagined.
